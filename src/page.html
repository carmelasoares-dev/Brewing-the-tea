        How this radar works, and what it can't see

        Stages. Emerging means creator counts are rising week on week, or the trend is under two weeks old. Peaking means trackers label it Peak, or it's at maximum participation. Holding means a format or platform shift with steady use. Declining means creator counts are falling or trackers label it Fading. Creator counts beat total views: a trend can keep piling up views long after people stop making it.

        Sources. Public trend trackers (Virlo, SocialPilot, SocialBee), platform newsrooms (Snap, Meta, LinkedIn, Pinterest) and industry research (Sprout Social, Brand24, Emplifi). Each card names its source and date.

        Blind spots. This isn't platform API data. TikTok and Instagram signals are strongest. Snapchat, Facebook, LinkedIn and Reddit signals are mostly platform announcements and research, not live trend counts. Most trackers are US-centric, so seasonal trends are flipped for Australia's summer. Single-source trends are marked Medium or Low confidence.

        Before it goes in a deck. Each card has a "Validate" line: the Brandwatch query or GWI cut that would confirm it with the agency's own tools.
